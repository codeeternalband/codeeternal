Biography

Progressive metal group Code Eternal is set to make waves once again with the release of their brand-new single “Brace Yourself” on October 10, 2025. This marks the first single from their long-awaited debut album.

Fronted by prodigious guitarist Juho Ranta-Maunus — who at just 14 years old won Guitar World Magazine’s “Young Guitarist of the Year” in 2020 — the band has been steadily building a reputation as one of Finland’s most promising new forces in metal. Founded in 2021, Code Eternal quickly turned heads with their 2022 debut EP Sixth Wave. Now, three years later, they return stronger, sharper, and more ambitious than ever.

“Brace Yourself” dives into thought-provoking territory, drawing inspiration from the concept of artificial intelligence evolving into a self-defending entity that could challenge humanity’s existence. Musically, the track is a dynamic blend of progressive metal, heavy riffs, and funk-infused grooves, elevated by pulsating synth layers that add depth and urgency to the band’s sound.

“This track really shows where we’re heading as a band,” says Ranta-Maunus. “It’s heavy, complex, but also has a groove and energy that feels alive.”

With their unique ability to fuse technical precision with raw intensity, Code Eternal are carving their own path in the modern metal landscape. “Brace Yourself” is only the beginning of what promises to be a standout debut album.

Code Eternal Lineup

Guitars – Juho Ranta-Maunus

Drums – Marlon Mäkelä

Brace Yourself will be available on all major streaming platforms October 10, 2025.
