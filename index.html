Biography

Code Eternal is a prog-influenced metal band from South Ostrobothnia, Finland. The band was founded in 2021 by award-winning guitarist Juho Ranta-Maunus, who was only 14 at the time. He gathered the best players he could find from his area, and in 2022 they released their debut EP Sixth Wave. Now, three years later they released the first single from their upcoming album called Brace Yourself. Code Eternal fuses prog metal, heavy metal, and funk into a fresh and powerful sound with rhythmic synths that add depth and momentum.
