Biography

Progressive metal group Code Eternal made waves with the release of their single “Brace Yourself” on October 10, 2025. It marks the first single from their long-awaited debut album.

Fronted by prodigious guitarist Juho Ranta-Maunus, the band has built a reputation as one of Finland’s most promising new forces in metal. Founded in 2021, Code Eternal quickly turned heads with their 2022 debut EP Sixth Wave.

Brace Yourself explores the possibility of AI evolving into a self-defending entity. Musically, the track blends progressive metal, heavy riffs, and funk-infused grooves with pulsating synth layers.

“This track really shows where we’re heading as a band,” says Ranta-Maunus.
